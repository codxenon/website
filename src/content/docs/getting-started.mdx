CoD Xe adds mod support and community-made experiences to supported Xbox 360
games. It runs on modified Xbox 360 hardware and through Xenia on Windows.

<Aside type="caution" title="IMPORTANT">

- We **do not support** piracy
- We **do not support** cheating in online games

</Aside>

## Setup Overview

1. Check that your game and Media ID are supported.
2. Prepare a clean copy of your game files.
3. Follow the setup path for Xbox 360 or Xenia.
4. Install the title update required by your game or mod.
5. Install CoD Xe.
6. Continue with a game-specific guide.
