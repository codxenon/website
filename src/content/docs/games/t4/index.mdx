## Single-player settings

In Campaign or Zombies, pause the game and open **Options** > **Extra Settings**.

- **Field of View** adjusts how much of the scene you can see.
- **Split-screen Layout** switches between **Side by Side** and **Top / Bottom** during a split-screen match.
